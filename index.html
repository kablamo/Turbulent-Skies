✈ Turbulent Skies

You're the only crew member on Flight 404. Get your passengers and their luggage from the terminal onto the plane, take off, and keep this rattling propeller plane in one piece until you land.

  • Move: WASD · Look: mouse or arrow keys · Sprint: Shift · Jump: Space · Pause: P · All controls: H
  • On a touch screen: use the stick at bottom-left to move, drag anywhere to look, and tap the buttons at bottom-right. Tap ? for all controls.
  • Interact / pick up: look at something and press E (or click). While carrying, E throws and Q puts it down.
  • Vehicles: press E on the shuttle bus or the baggage truck to drive it. W/S drive, A/D steer, F does the vehicle's job, and E gets out.
  • Passengers wait in the terminal. Park the bus by the terminal doors and press F to board them, then park by the plane's cargo ramp and press F to let them off.
  • Luggage comes out on the conveyor belt. Park the truck's bed at the end of the belt so the bags drop onto it, drive to the plane, then pick each bag off the truck (E) and carry it up the ramp into the cargo bay.
  • In the air: sit in the captain's seat to fly (A/D steer, W/S climb and dive). Fix emergencies from the list at bottom-left, and keep the passengers happy.
